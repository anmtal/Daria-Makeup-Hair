The Artist

Hi, I'm Daria — your calm on the morning of the big day.

I'm a bridal makeup & hair artist based in Charlotte, North Carolina, with a passion for effortless, soft-glam looks that feel like you — only more luminous. No heavy layers, no masks. Just skin that glows, eyes that read beautifully in every photo, and hair that holds from the first look to the last dance.

I've spent years perfecting a natural-beauty approach for brides, editorials and photoshoots — and I travel for destination weddings across the Carolinas and beyond. When I'm not with a bride, I teach the craft to up-and-coming artists.
